WAREHOUSE DAILY OPERATIONS GUIDE (2024)
========================================

SECTION 1: SHIFT SCHEDULES & WORKFLOW
-------------------------------------
- Morning Shift: 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM
- Afternoon Shift: 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM
- Night Shift: 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM
- All workers must sign in 10 minutes before their shift and attend the team briefing.
- Daily workload is divided into receiving, inventory update, picking, packing, and dispatch.

SECTION 2: INVENTORY HANDLING
-----------------------------
- Use handheld scanners for all inventory movements.
- Report discrepancies immediately to the Inventory Supervisor.
- Damaged goods must be placed in the “Inspection Zone” before tagging.
- Shelves are labeled using alphanumeric codes (e.g., A1-B3).

SECTION 3: EQUIPMENT USAGE & MAINTENANCE
----------------------------------------
- Pallet jacks and forklifts must be returned to charging docks after shift.
- Battery water levels should be checked every Monday morning.
- Only trained staff may operate powered equipment (see list in Appendix A).
- Monthly maintenance logs must be filled by the Maintenance Lead.

SECTION 4: CLEANLINESS & WASTE DISPOSAL
---------------------------------------
- Floors must be swept at the end of each shift.
- Spillages must be reported and cleaned immediately using absorbent pads.
- Recycling bins are color-coded:
  - Blue: Paper/Cardboard
  - Green: Plastic
  - Red: Hazardous waste
- All cleaning supplies are stored in Room C-14.

SECTION 5: SECURITY & ACCESS CONTROL
-------------------------------------
- Entry to the warehouse requires ID badge scans.
- All doors auto-lock after 8:00 PM.
- Visitors must be logged in at the front desk and accompanied at all times.
- Security footage is retained for 30 days and reviewed weekly.

SECTION 6: INCIDENT REPORTING & FEEDBACK
----------------------------------------
- Any injuries, equipment damage, or process violations must be reported within 1 hour.
- Use Form WHS-207 and submit to the Shift Supervisor.
- Anonymous feedback can be left in the box outside Room B-2 or submitted via the internal portal.

WAREHOUSE DAILY OPERATIONS GUIDE (2025)
========================================
SECTION 1: SHIFT TIMINGS
Morning Shift: 6:00 AM – 2:00 PM
Afternoon Shift: 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM
Night Shift: 10:00 PM – 6:00 AM
All employees must clock in no later than 5 minutes before shift start,

SECTION 2: PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
Safety Helmets – Required at all times inside loading areas.
High-Visibility Vests – Fluorescent yellow or orange with reflective stripes.
Steel-Toed Boots – Must conform to ISO 20345:2011.
Cut-Resistant Gloves – Mandatory when handling sharp materials.
Hearing Protection – Required in areas above 85 dB

SECTION 3: FIRE SAFETY & EVACUATION PROCEDURES
Fire Extinguishers: One every 15 meters; monthly inspection.
Emergency Exits: At least two per zone, clearly marked.
Evacuation Drills: Every 6 months; missed drills require retraining.
Assembly Points: 50 meters from building, marked with green signage.
Never use elevators during evacuation.


SECTION 4: VISITOR PROTOCOL
All visitors must sign in at the security desk and wear visitor badges.
Visitors must be escorted by a warehouse employee at all times.
PPE is required for visitors entering the warehouse floor.
Visits should be scheduled at least 24 hours in advance.

SECTION 5: MATERIAL HANDLING AND STORAGE
Forklift operators must be certified and recertified every 2 years.
Do not exceed 1500 kg per shelving level unless labeled otherwise.
Use Class 2 ratchet straps for loads over 200 kg.
Hazardous chemicals must be stored in locked ventilated cabinets.

SECTION 6: FIRST AID AND INCIDENT REPORTING
First Aid Kits must be:
Available every 30 meters.
Checked weekly by a safety officer.
Incident Reports:
Must be submitted within 2 hours using Form WHS-101.
Use the internal safety portal for submission.
Emergency contacts are listed on the main notice board and in Section 9.

SECTION 7: TRAINING AND CERTIFICATION
Safety Training is required:
Annually for all employees.
Immediately after any safety violation or accident.
Lockout/Tagout procedures must be reviewed every 12 months.


SECTION 8: EMERGENCY CONTACTS
Fire Department: 101
Police: 100
On-site Medical Officer: Ext. 221
Warehouse Safety Lead: Ext. 115
