WAREHOUSE SAFETY MANUAL (2025)
==================================================

SECTION 1: PERSONAL PROTECTIVE EQUIPMENT (PPE)
--------------------------------------------------
- Safety Helmets – Protect against falling objects and head injuries.
- High-Visibility Vests – Must be fluorescent yellow or orange with retro-reflective stripes.
- Steel-Toed Boots – Must conform to ISO 20345:2011 standard.
- Cut-Resistant Gloves – Level 3 or higher EN388-rated gloves must be worn during handling sharp or metal objects.
- Hearing Protection – Required in zones exceeding 85 dB (e.g., near industrial compressors).

SECTION 2: FIRE SAFETY EQUIPMENT AND EXIT PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------------
1. Fire Extinguishers must be located every 15 meters in accessible positions and inspected monthly.
2. Fire Blankets should be located in areas with welding or flammable liquids.
3. Sprinkler System: Must comply with NFPA 13 standards and be tested quarterly.
4. Emergency Exits:
   - Clearly marked with illuminated signs.
   - Unobstructed with 1.2 meters of clearance.
   - At least 2 exits per zone, within 30 meters.
5. Evacuation Protocol:
   - Stop work immediately on alarm.
   - Leave belongings and use the nearest fire exit.
   - Never use elevators or powered lifts.
6. Assembly Points:
   - At least 50 meters from building.
   - Green "Assembly Zone" signage.
   - Roll call by Safety Officer within 10 minutes.
7. Drill Frequency: Every 6 months; missed drills require safety retraining.
8. Emergency lights: Minimum 90-minute functionality after power cut.

SECTION 3: MATERIAL HANDLING SAFETY
--------------------------------------------------
- Forklift Operators: Certified; recertification every 2 years.
- Load Limits: Do not exceed 1500 kg per level unless labeled otherwise.
- Straps and Bindings: Use Class 2 ratchet straps for loads over 200 kg.

SECTION 4: CHEMICAL AND HAZARDOUS MATERIALS HANDLING
--------------------------------------------------
1. Storage:
   - Ventilated, locked cabinets.
   - At least 10 meters from ignition sources.
   - Accessible MSDS sheets.
2. Labeling:
   - GHS pictograms, chemical name, date of receipt and expiry.
3. Spill Containment:
   - Kits within 15 meters of storage.
   - Include absorbent pads, neutralizers, and disposal bags.
4. PPE:
   - Nitrile gloves, face shield, apron.
   - Respirators for volatile/corrosive materials.
5. Disposal:
   - Follow EPA Regulation CFR 261.
   - Log entries in WHS-204 Chemical Logbook.

SECTION 5: LOCKOUT/TAGOUT (LOTO) PROCEDURES
--------------------------------------------------
1. Authorization:
   - Only certified technicians.
   - Certification valid for 3 years.
2. Lockout Procedure:
   1. Notify affected employees.
   2. Shut down equipment.
   3. Isolate energy source.
   4. Lock and tag energy points.
   5. Verify isolation.
3. Tagout Guidelines:
   - Include technician name, date/time, reason.
   - Only technician who tagged can remove it.
4. Training:
   - Annual requirement (every 12 months).
   - Emergency removal needs written supervisor approval.
5. Covered Equipment:
   - Conveyor belts, hydraulic presses, forklift charging stations.

SECTION 6: SAFETY SIGNAGE AND INCIDENT REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------
- Hazardous Zones: Marked with red-yellow striping.
- Pedestrian Walkways: Green/white markings.
- MSDS Locations: Within 10 meters of chemical zones.
- Reporting:
   - All incidents reported within 2 hours.
   - Use Form WHS-101 or internal safety portal.

SECTION 7: PPE REPLACEMENT FREQUENCY
--------------------------------------------------
